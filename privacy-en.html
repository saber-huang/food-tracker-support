Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This app helps you log what you eat and estimate calories and nutrition. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it, and how you can delete it.

The Short Version

  • Your profile and food log are stored only on your phone.
  • Photos or nutrition targets are sent online only when you use an AI feature, and the app asks for your consent before the first time.
  • No account is needed. There are no ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.

Information Stored on Your Phone

  • Profile: sex, age, height, weight, activity level and goal.
  • Food log: the name, weight, calories and nutrients of each food you log.
  • Settings and preferences: your calorie hint setting, the tastes, allergies, foods to avoid and cooking time you enter, and your most recent recipes.

All of this stays on your phone and is not stored on this app's server. Only when you use an AI feature is part of it sent with the request, as described in the next section. If you use iCloud Backup, it is included in your iPhone backup in your own iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Information Sent When You Use AI Features

Photo scanning, recipe generation and next-meal suggestions are powered by Claude, an AI from Anthropic. Before you use these features for the first time, the app explains this and asks for your consent. Once you agree, each use sends:

  • Photo scan: the meal photo you choose. It is resized and re-compressed before upload, without the location or other photo details.
  • Recipe generation: your goal (lose fat, maintain or build muscle), your daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets, and the tastes, allergies, foods to avoid and cooking time you entered.
  • Next-meal suggestion: the same as recipe generation, except the targets are scaled to that one meal based on what's left of today's calories.

Not sent: your age, height, weight or the food log itself, and nothing that identifies you, such as your name, phone number or location.

This information goes to this app's server (hosted on Render), which passes it to Anthropic for processing. Results are returned only to your phone.

How the Server and Third Parties Handle It

  • This app's server does not store your photos or what you typed. It uses them only to handle that request.
  • To answer identical requests faster and avoid counting them twice, generated recipes are kept in the server's memory for up to 24 hours.
  • Anthropic processes this data under its commercial terms: by default it is not used to train models, and inputs and outputs are generally deleted within 30 days. Content flagged for violating its usage policy may be kept for up to 2 years, and data may be kept longer where required by law. See the Anthropic Privacy Center.
  • The hosting provider, Render, keeps logs with technical details such as request times, endpoints and network addresses, used to troubleshoot problems and prevent abuse. They are kept for only a few days (currently 3).

Usage Counts

To control costs and prevent abuse, the server counts how many times each device and each network address uses AI features per day:

  • The device ID is randomly generated the first time the app runs, is stored only in this phone's keychain, and is not linked to your identity. If you delete and reinstall the app, it usually stays the same, so daily counts don't reset on reinstall.
  • These counts exist only in the server's memory and clear automatically after about 24 hours. They also clear whenever the server restarts or sleeps.

What We Don't Do

  • We don't collect names, phone numbers or email addresses, and no account is needed.
  • There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent your data.

Your Choices

  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw it anytime on the Profile tab. You'll be asked again before your next use of an AI feature. Manual logging isn't affected.
  • Delete your data: choose Delete All Data on the Profile tab to delete your profile, food log and settings on this phone. This can't be undone. Usage counts on the server clear automatically after about 24 hours.
  • Don't use AI features: without consent, you can still log meals manually.

Who This App Is For

The app's formulas are based on research in adults. It is intended only for adults 18 and older and is not directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a significant way, the date above will be updated. If what's sent to the AI changes, the app will ask for your consent again.
